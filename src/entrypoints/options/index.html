Turbobulls Connector

Custom data sources

Prices from your own market-data provider, with your own API key. Your keys stay in this browser, on this device.

Set up in three steps

    Your API keys

    For the providers you pay for

    Your own sources

    Any https address that answers JSON or CSV, such as a pension fund or a sheet you keep

    What Turbobulls sees

    • Turbobulls never sees your keys, or the addresses of your own sources: only their names, and the prices.
    • This extension cannot read any web page, Turbobulls included. Chrome asks you before it may reach a new site.
