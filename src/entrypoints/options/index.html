Your API keys

For the providers you pay for

Provider names belong to their owners and only show which services the Connector can ask with your key. Turbobulls is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

Your own sources

Any https address that answers JSON or CSV

Nobody else sees your keys or your data

Your keys stay here

In this browser, on this device. They go only to your provider; Turbobulls never receives them.

Turbobulls cannot read your data

The prices, and which assets you price, are saved end-to-end encrypted with the rest of your portfolio.

Names, never addresses

Turbobulls in your browser gets the names of your own sources and the prices, not where they come from.

No access to web pages

The extension cannot read any page, Turbobulls included. Chrome asks you before it may reach a new site.
