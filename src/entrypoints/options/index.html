How it works

  • Paste the key of the provider you pay for, or add a source of your own. Chrome asks once before the extension may reach a new site.
  • In Turbobulls, add a custom asset and choose From my data provider.
  • Turbobulls asks this extension for prices; the extension asks your provider and passes back only the prices.
  • Turbobulls never sees your keys. This extension cannot read any web page, Turbobulls included.
